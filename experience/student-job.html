Student assistant (vacataire)

Close-up: Espace Monde

Context

Espace Monde is the University of Rouen's welcome desk for international students. Those who come through Campus France without an exchange programme go there to enrol, sort out their paperwork and settle in, and most of them arrive in the same few weeks of September.

What I took from it

In this job, you have to work on how you communicate, be patient and genuinely want to help.

What I did

  • A one-day training on the Saphire software in July 2026
  • Welcoming students at the front desk
  • Helping them put together their administrative enrolment file
  • Creating the files, on paper and digitally
  • Checking the files

Skills used

  • Oral communication
  • Intercultural communication
  • Professional English
  • Active listening
  • Handling a steady flow of people
  • Administrative procedures
  • Adaptability
  • Teamwork
