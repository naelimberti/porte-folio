Context

The SNEE is a national status for students who want to build a business project during their studies. In Normandy, it goes through Pépite Normandie. I got it in July 2025.

What I took from it

This status is what pushed me towards business development and sales, with a real interest in responsible entrepreneurship.

What I did

  • Silva Edita: a rooftop greening project, awarded at Proj'Impact 2026.
  • Naillies: six months with Léa Portmann in Les Jeunes Pousses, building a business plan in the beauty sector.
